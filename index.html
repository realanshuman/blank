Markdown, rendered as you type

No preview pane, no split screen. Formatting appears inline the moment you type it, and one switch turns all of it off for a completely uniform page.

Focus on one sentence

Dim everything except the line, sentence or paragraph you are in. Typewriter scrolling keeps that line at a fixed height instead of sinking to the bottom.

Turn off the backspace key

For when editing is the thing stopping you. Deleting, cutting and undo are all blocked, not just the one key, so the only way out is forward.
